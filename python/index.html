Four simple ways to go greener

♻️

Use less, reuse more

Choose durable items, repair what you can, and give useful things a second life.

🥬

Eat with the seasons

Enjoy seasonal produce and plan meals to help reduce avoidable food waste.

🚲

Travel thoughtfully

Walk, cycle, carpool, or take public transit when it works for your journey.

🌱

Grow something

Plant native flowers or herbs to make room for pollinators and local wildlife.
